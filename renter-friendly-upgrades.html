9 renter-friendly upgrades that do not require drilling

A rental can feel more finished without becoming permanent. These upgrades focus on reversible changes that improve lighting, storage and visual order while keeping commitment low.

The best renter-friendly upgrades solve a real annoyance without creating a bigger problem at move-out. Think removable mounting, freestanding storage, rechargeable lighting and finishes that can be tested before they go everywhere.

Always follow the product instructions and test adhesives or removable finishes in a hidden area first. Wall paint, texture, humidity and installation quality can all affect removal.
